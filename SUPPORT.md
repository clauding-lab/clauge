# Clauge — Support

Clauge is a free, read-only app for viewing your own Claude usage — on **Mac** (a menu-bar app) and **iPhone** (a companion app). It is an independent, unofficial companion and is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Anthropic.**

## Common questions

**How do I connect my account on iPhone?**
Open the app and tap **Connect Claude.ai**, then sign in to your own claude.ai account with an **email magic code** or **Sign in with Apple**. "Continue with Google" does not work inside in-app browsers — please use email or Apple.

**I just want to see what it looks like first.**
On the first screen, tap **"Take a look with sample data"** to explore the whole app with clearly-labelled example numbers — no account needed.

**Why is the Analytics tab empty on my iPhone?**
The Analytics tab mirrors your Claude Code analytics from the **Clauge Mac app**. Install Clauge for Mac (free, Mac App Store) and sign in under the **same Apple ID**; your analytics then sync to your iPhone automatically through your own private iCloud. Without the Mac app, the Usage and Trends tabs still work on their own.

**Does Clauge collect my data?**
No. Clauge has no server and no account system — it reads only your own data and shows it to you. See the [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/clauding-lab/clauge/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).

**Does Clauge cost anything?**
No. Both apps are free, with no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

**Is Clauge made by Anthropic?**
No. Clauge is an independent, unofficial companion built by Adnan Rashid. It is not affiliated with Anthropic.
